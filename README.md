A one-star review written by a houseplant about its human owner: watered once a month under the name of "tough love", described to dates as "so easy to keep alive", while the plant up there photosynthesizes out of spite.

> One star. He waters me once a month, calls it 'tough love', and tells his dates I'm 'so easy to keep alive' while I'm up here photosynthesizing out of spite.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the review to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 279 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
